# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Mostly local customers in Bangladesh, a mixed audience: mainly men buying panjabis for occasions (Eid, weddings, events), plus some everyday and gift purchases. Many browse on phones.

## Product Purpose

ARZ (arzlifestyle.com) is a menswear brand's Shopify store, selling panjabis and related traditional wear, priced in BDT. It exists so customers can discover collections, pick a size with confidence, and order through either Shopify checkout or WhatsApp. Success is a completed order on either channel.

## Positioning

- **Premium craftsmanship:** embroidery (including zardozi), fabric and finishing quality.
- **Bespoke tailoring:** custom-tailored orders from a catalogue of numbered design codes (the "Tailored Bespoke" page).
- **Accessible luxury:** a luxury feel at roughly 4,000–15,000 BDT.

## Operating Context

- Ordering is split about evenly between Shopify checkout and WhatsApp (+880 1341630198). Both paths must stay first-class.
- Seasonal campaigns (Eid/Ramadan popup and decorations) and named collection drops (e.g. "Threads of Eden").
- Fixed-amount automatic discounts per product line, applied via discount codes at checkout.
- Order tracking page; prepayment popup; free shipping over 10,000 BDT.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme, hand-written Liquid and vanilla JS/CSS (no build step), in this repo. Theme-editor settings live in `config/settings_data.json` and can drift from the live store.
- Size charts by product type (panjabi, pants, shacket) with fit specs per size.
- Custom-tailored form submits through Web3Forms, imgbb uploads and a Discord webhook. These are client-side keys, so they are public.
- Sister brand Corso Velle (same owner, `E:\code\Corso-Velle-V2`) is a reference for engineering practices only. ARZ keeps its own identity.

## Brand Commitments

- Name "ARZ"; wordmark logo in brown (#3D2500) and a light variant (`assets/arz logo*.svg`, set in the header).
- The owner has explicitly said ARZ must stay unique and not adopt Corso Velle's look.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real product photography and product videos on Shopify; custom-tailored catalogue images.
- No testimonials, reviews, press or customer counts in the theme. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Craft first: let fabric, embroidery and fit details lead. Show the garment, don't decorate around it.
2. Two equal ways to buy: checkout and WhatsApp are both primary paths.
3. Confidence in fit: sizing information has to be clear and easy to reach.
4. Premium but approachable: luxury cues without making the prices feel out of reach.
5. Phone-first: most visits happen on mobile.
